PATIENT INFO — FINAL ENGLISH ENGINEERING DEMO
Packaged: 16 November 2026

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder on your computer.
2. Double-click index.html to open the English demo in a desktop browser.
3. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

Viewing requires no internet connection, server, development tools or font installation.
The default device canvas is 1024 x 600. Demo controls are below it.
If the window is too small, clear Actual Size to fit the demo to the window.

IMPORTANT DEMO BEHAVIOR
Export and Clear Device writes the complete backup to the simulated USB,
then clears the active device's saved patient and treatment records.
Failed export retains existing data. Import replaces the complete device archive
after confirmation; it does not merge records.

USB B starts with two fictional patients and one treatment record per patient.
Choose USB B under Simulated drive, then open Patient Info > USB Transfer >
Import from USB > Replace and Import. The device then contains those two patients
and two records, replacing its original archive.

All device storage, USB drives and laser output are simulated in page memory.
Restart Demo Device preserves saved simulated archives. Refreshing or closing
this webpage resets the entire simulation. No real USB or laser is accessed.
Use fictional patient information only.
